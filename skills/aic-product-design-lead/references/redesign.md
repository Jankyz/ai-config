# Redesign mode

Redesign mode may question the existing visual language, including visual direction, palette, typography, spacing,
radius, surfaces, elevation, component language, navigation, screen composition, information hierarchy, interaction
patterns, and tokens.

Begin with visual-direction discovery. Unless the user explicitly authorizes autonomous selection, develop three
meaningfully different, product-specific directions before substantially implementing the redesign. Do not present minor
color variations as different directions.

For each direction, describe:

- concept and personality, and why it suits the product;
- palette and typography direction;
- density and layout philosophy;
- component and interaction character;
- useful references or established patterns; and
- material risks and tradeoffs.

Use Product Design visual ideation when it materially helps. Do not begin a substantial implementation until the user
selects a direction, unless they explicitly authorize the agent to choose one.

After selection, establish or update `DESIGN.md` with durable principles, define the required tokens and component
rules, then implement incrementally. Visually QA each major surface against the chosen direction across representative
widths and states, and fix meaningful deviations before completion.
