# Planning standard

Create a written implementation plan before any material change. The plan is an execution contract, not a rough idea.

It records the goal, current state, requirements, confirmed decisions, constraints, scope boundaries, affected areas, sequence, testing, risks, rollback or recovery, definition of done, and open decisions.

Resolve independently answerable facts before requesting owner input. Material open decisions block implementation until the owner resolves them. A plan is a draft until explicitly approved for implementation.

Keep plans proportional to risk and change size. Do not use a plan to silently authorize unrelated redesign or future work.
